## A. Audience Calibration

The immediate target for this revision is startup/API/GPU/cloud credit applications and related accelerator discussions:

- Google for Startups Cloud
- Microsoft for Startups
- AWS Activate
- NVIDIA Inception
- Anthropic startup support / API credits
- OpenAI startup/referral pathways
- UAE accelerator/pilot-support channels (Hub71 / AI ecosystem)

These programmes evaluate:

- Is this a real technical project with internal capability?
- Is the product scope meaningful and well-understood by the founder?
- What specifically will credits enable?
- Is there a credible path from validation to real use?
- Is the privacy/responsible-use model serious?

They do **not** require equity-investor data:
- TAM/SAM/SOM numerical claims
- Revenue, funding history, or runway
- Exit potential or comparable transaction analysis
- External customer testimonials
- Signed production deployment

This revision therefore implements founder credibility, differentiation, roadmap, and commercial pathway sections while deliberately **not fabricating** investor-focused financial or market-size claims.

## E. No-Fabrication Confirmation

The following items will **not** be created or implied in any public material as a result of this revision:

- No customer testimonials (none exist)
- No external production deployment claim (development-stage only)
- No revenue, pricing, or subscription model (not established)
- No funding history or runway figures
- No TAM/SAM/SOM numerical claims
- No exit potential or comparable transaction wording
- No guaranteed productivity improvement claim
- No 1.5-year reduction or 14-day delivery claim
- No independent certification or compliance approval by AI

All claims in the public revision are either internally validated, framed as development-stage, or explicitly qualified as planned/preparing.
