# AxiOMSphere

> AI assistance for Asset Integrity Management System development before production begins.

**Website:** [axiomsphereai.com](https://axiomsphereai.com)
**Contact:** [hello@axiomsphereai.com](mailto:hello@axiomsphereai.com)

## Purpose

AxiOMSphere is a development-stage product for industrial project teams and engineers establishing Asset Integrity Management Systems (AIMS) before production begins.

It begins with on-demand support for preparing and reviewing AIMS governing documents and related work products. Its development direction expands through controlled capability layers toward engineer- and client-facing support during AIMS development and project readiness.

## Current Applied Capability

**On-Demand AIMS Document Preparation and Review**

AxiOMSphere currently supports specialists in preparing and reviewing structured AIMS governing documents and related work products. Technical content and approval remain with the accountable specialist.
