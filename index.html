Make the document lifecycle easier to govern

Trust & governance

ISO 55001 requirementsISO 55002 guidanceGFMAM Asset Management Landscape reference

Project source documents remain on private infrastructure. Where external evaluation is authorised, only anonymised generated outputs or de-identified evaluation context may be submitted for assessment of structure and framework alignment, without project-identifying information.

Human approval remains authoritative. Outputs are intended to be traceable to their governed basis, and sensitive project information can remain within an authorised private environment.

Operational readiness

Connect documentation with the organisation that must use it

AxiOMSphere is designed to help teams connect documentation with readiness: roles, responsibilities, dependencies, handover knowledge and the evidence needed for a clearer management review as a project moves toward commissioning and operations.

Project-to-operations continuity

Evidence for management review

Human-governed support
