The Opportunity

The problem we are solving.

Industrial projects must begin building their Asset Integrity Management System before production starts — at the stage where scope, roles, processes and operating philosophy must be defined and where the consequences of gaps are most severe.

The gap today

  • Early AIMS decisions are made without structured AI assistance, creating gaps that are costly to close once capital is committed
  • Scope misalignments and procedure inconsistencies accumulate across disciplines and surface late — when changes carry the highest impact on schedule and capital
  • Sensitive source materials — technical standards, asset data, proprietary procedures, regulatory requirements — cannot be submitted to external AI services
  • No interactive system supports key specialists in real time with intelligent guidance through fragmented standardisation sources

What AxiOMSphere provides

  • AI-assisted creation, review and traceability of AIMS work products — running entirely on private GPU infrastructure
  • Source documents remain on-premise throughout; external evaluation services receive only anonymised context
  • Every output is reviewed for ISO 55001/55002 alignment before an engineer approves it for operational use
  • Staged development roadmap from current applied capability toward a full project lifecycle support platform

Platform Foundation

AIMS: from project startup to organised production operations.

Asset Integrity Management System (AIMS) is the integrated framework connecting project startup, operational readiness, asset integrity, reliability, maintenance, assurance, and continuous improvement.

01
Project Definition
Strategy, scope, objectives and stakeholder requirements.
02
Organisational Setup
Roles, responsibilities, interfaces and governance structures.
03
Functional Framework
Policies, plans, procedures, registers and operational controls.
04
Operational Readiness
Asset integrity, reliability, maintenance, assurance and handover preparation.
05
Production Operations
Controlled execution, monitoring, feedback and continuous improvement.
AxiOMSphere is designed to connect these stages through specialised agents and a maintained single source of truth for the project's AIMS documentation.

Framework References

Built on recognised AIMS standards.

The AIMS work-product pipeline is structured around three core framework references.

ISO 55001:2024
Asset management — requirements
The international requirements standard for asset management systems. Defines the mandatory elements of an AIMS and the evidence required for conformance.
ISO 55002:2018
Asset management — guidelines
Guidance on the application of ISO 55001. Informs work-product structure, content requirements, and review criteria used throughout the pipeline.
GFMAM Landscape
Asset Management Landscape
39 subjects across 6 subject groups — applied as a reference for scope and completeness assessment across AIMS work-product categories.

M1 — Current Applied Stage

What the system does today.

Validated in our development environment on private GPU infrastructure. All outputs require qualified engineer review before operational use.

📝
Work-Product Draft Generation
Engineer submits a plain-language request via Telegram. The system generates a structured AIMS work-product draft — a procedure, register, plan, or other applicable work product — aligned to ISO 55001 requirements and ISO 55002 guidance.
🔍
Framework-Alignment Review
Every draft is reviewed for alignment with ISO 55001 requirements and ISO 55002 guidance. Gaps are identified and structured revision guidance is generated for each review cycle.
🔄
Structured Revision Loop
Score-gated revision cycle: draft → review → revise → re-review. Outputs progress through multiple passes. Engineers review and approve before operational use — no autonomous acceptance decisions.
📂
Document Registry & Traceability
Accepted documents are registered in a local registry with generation metadata — revision count, timestamp, review record — enabling audit trail and traceability.
🔒
Privacy-First Infrastructure
Runs on private GPU infrastructure. All document content stays on-premise. No project data is sent to external services during draft generation. Evaluation service access is optional and configurable.
🧠
Evidence Retention for Development
Validated outputs are retained as structured evidence. This evidence base is intended to support future controlled improvement cycles — a development objective under M2.

Built for responsible industrial use.

AxiOMSphere develops and reviews AIMS management-system work products in accordance with ISO 55001 requirements and ISO 55002 guidance. Generated outputs and recommendations require qualified human review before operational use and do not replace formal assurance or certification activities.

Sensitive source information remains private by default. Approved external services receive anonymised context only unless separately authorised. Learning is not automatic — no agent updates its behaviour without a human development decision.
