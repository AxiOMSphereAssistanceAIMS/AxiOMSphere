About the Platform

What Is AxiOMSphere

AxiOMSphere is a development-stage full-stack, closed-loop industrial AI platform that coordinates specialised agents to build, synchronise, restore and continuously improve the integrated AIMS operating framework required for industrial projects and production operations. The platform is designed to operate entirely on private infrastructure — source materials never leave the local environment during drafting.

The platform is structured in accordance with ISO 55001 requirements and ISO 55002 guidance as its AIMS management-system framework basis. It uses the GFMAM Asset Management Landscape as a broader process-framework reference. All outputs are recommendations — qualified human review is required before any generated work product is used operationally.

The Challenge

The Problem

Early project decisions shape the future operating organisation.

Industrial projects must begin building their Asset Integrity Management System (AIMS) before production starts. The highest risk arises during pilot development and before the full-field investment decision — when scope, configuration, roles, processes and operating philosophy must be defined. These are decisions that are difficult and costly to revise once full capital is deployed.

Without a structured AIMS framework in place from the outset, scope gaps, interface misalignments and procedure inconsistencies accumulate across disciplines and remain undetected. When they surface late, they drive costly revision waves at the stage where changes carry the highest impact on schedule and capital.

Sensitive source materials — technical standards, asset data, proprietary procedures, regulatory requirements — cannot be routinely submitted to external AI services during this process.

The Approach

The Solution

A private, coordinated environment for building and maintaining AIMS.

AxiOMSphere is a development-stage full-stack, closed-loop industrial AI platform that coordinates specialised agents on private infrastructure to build, synchronise, restore and continuously improve the AIMS operating framework — from project startup through production operations.

The platform operates privately by default: source materials stay on private infrastructure throughout the drafting workflow; approved external services receive only anonymized context for benchmark evaluation.

Platform Architecture

Full-stack, closed-loop capability architecture.

AxiOMSphere is designed as an integrated stack — every layer contributes to a closed loop of build, supervise, restore and improve. Layers 1–3 face the AIMS framework directly; layers 4–8 keep the system operating reliably under real project conditions.

Layer 1
Project Development
Drafts, reviews and registers AIMS work products aligned to ISO 55001 requirements and ISO 55002 guidance across all five lifecycle stages.
Layer 2
Master Knowledge & Document Base
Maintains a structured, searchable Single Source of Truth for AIMS work products, evidence records, reference standards and validated outputs.
Layer 3
Orchestration & Synchronisation
Coordinates specialised agents across the framework, resolves interface conflicts, detects scope gaps and maintains consistency across the full document set.
Layer 4
Execution Control & Feedback
Monitors workflow execution, tracks quality thresholds and routes outputs through structured human review gates before any operational use.
Layer 5
Learning & Training
Captures validated outputs and preference pairs to continuously improve agent generation quality through an automated fine-tuning cycle.
Layer 6
Recovery & Repair
Detects infrastructure failures, diagnoses root causes and applies targeted repairs — keeping the AIMS development workflow operational without manual intervention.
Layer 7
Security, Policy & Oversight
Enforces access controls, change management gates and audit trails. No change passes to production without policy-layer approval.
Layer 8
Resilience & Redundancy
Dual-node routing, failover logic and health monitoring ensure continuity of AIMS development workflows under hardware or network constraints.

Example Validation Scenario

Pilot development before a full-field investment decision.

The following scenario illustrates how AxiOMSphere is designed to support AIMS framework development during the highest-value decision window — before full-field capital is committed.

Scenario: AIMS framework development for a pilot project approaching investment gate
Situation

A pilot project is approaching a full-field investment decision. The AIMS operating framework — roles, processes, procedures, registers and controls — must be sufficiently developed to support investment confidence. The project team is operating under time and resource constraints, with source materials distributed across engineering disciplines and subject to ongoing scope change.

Workflow
  • 1Source materials (technical standards, asset specifications, regulatory requirements) are ingested into a controlled private knowledge layer — no raw content transmitted to external services.
  • 2Work product drafts are generated across priority AIMS lifecycle stages, structured in accordance with ISO 55001 requirements and ISO 55002 guidance, with interfaces between documents maintained consistently.
  • 3A change event is submitted — a revised equipment specification affecting maintenance philosophy and spare parts strategy. The platform identifies downstream impact across affected procedures, registers and plans, and prepares a structured impact summary with supporting evidence for specialist review.
  • 4The impact summary is reviewed and approved by the qualified specialist. Affected work products are revised, re-registered and the knowledge base is updated with the validated outcome and lessons learned.
  • 5The process repeats as further scope decisions are made — each iteration feeding approved outputs back into the knowledge base to improve subsequent drafts and impact assessments.
Expected Validation Output
A review-ready AIMS work-product package — scope-complete, interface-aligned, evidence-referenced and structured for qualified human review — produced without accumulated unresolved gaps. Design outcome target: a 24-hour review cycle for a representative package. This is a target outcome of the planned controlled validation programme; it is not a guaranteed or currently demonstrated delivery time.

This scenario describes a design intent and planning-level validation target. No external pilot has been completed. Outcomes are subject to controlled validation.

Privacy Architecture

Private by design. Controlled evaluation only.

The information boundary is a fundamental design constraint, not an optional setting. Source materials stay local; only anonymized context reaches external evaluators.

Local — Private Infrastructure ON-PREM

  • All AIMS work-product generation and agent coordination
  • Source documents, project data and sensitive context
  • Document registry, evidence store and knowledge base
  • Model inference and fine-tuning pipeline
  • Audit trail and traceable source references

External — Controlled Evaluation Only ANONYMIZED

  • Benchmark review of anonymized agent output quality
  • Standards and guidance discovery from approved sources
  • Evaluator comparison using anonymized workflow context
  • No raw source documents or identifiable project data
Privacy commitment: Sensitive source information remains on private infrastructure by default. Approved external services receive anonymized context only unless separately authorised by the project owner.
