Current Applied Stage

What the system does today — validated in our development environment on private GPU infrastructure.

📝

Work-Product Draft Generation

Engineer submits a plain-language request via Telegram. The system generates a structured AIMS work-product draft — a procedure, register, plan, or other applicable work product — aligned to applicable ISO 55001 requirements and ISO 55002 guidance.

🔍

Framework-Alignment Review

Every draft is reviewed for alignment with applicable ISO 55001 requirements and ISO 55002 guidance. Gaps are identified and structured revision guidance is generated for each review cycle.

🔄

Structured Revision Loop

Structured revision cycle: draft → review → revise → re-review. Outputs progress through multiple passes. Engineers review and approve before operational use — no autonomous acceptance decisions.

📂

Document Registry & Traceability

Accepted documents are registered in a local registry with generation metadata — revision count, timestamp, review record — enabling audit trail and traceability.

🔒

Privacy-First Infrastructure

Runs on private GPU infrastructure. All document content stays on-premise. No project data is sent to external services during draft generation. Evaluation service access is optional and configurable.

🧠

Evidence Retention for Development

Validated outputs are retained as structured evidence. This evidence base is intended to support future controlled improvement cycles — a development objective under M2 (Controlled Knowledge and Self-Learning).

The Problem We Are Solving

The gap is not knowledge — it is access and consistency.

At the project justification stage, there is a critical need for empirical data, guiding documentation, and foundational decisions that will shape the project's development. These early choices can ultimately determine either the failure or the economic success of the enterprise.

In practice, organizations often proceed with launching low-budget pilot versions of projects. Limited resources frequently result in poor-quality documentation and suboptimal outcomes. While project execution methodologies have long been established, success largely depends on how consistently and accurately teams follow clear procedural steps to build an integrated project management system.

Despite the existence of fully standardized processes, there is still no interactive system capable of supporting key specialists in real time — providing intelligent guidance and acting as a companion that can efficiently navigate extensive, widely published yet fragmented standardization sources.

Artificial intelligence can address this gap by serving as a network of specialized agents, each designed for a specific purpose and trained on organization-specific documentation stored on corporate servers or within secure enterprise platforms.

The Challenge

The Problem

Early project decisions shape the future operating organisation.

Industrial projects must begin building their Asset Integrity Management System (AIMS) before production starts. The highest risk arises during pilot development and before the full-field investment decision — when scope, configuration, roles, processes and operating philosophy must be defined. These are decisions that are difficult and costly to revise once full capital is deployed.

Without a structured AIMS framework in place from the outset, scope gaps, interface misalignments and procedure inconsistencies accumulate across disciplines and remain undetected. When they surface late, they drive costly revision waves at the stage where changes carry the highest impact on schedule and capital.

Sensitive source materials — technical standards, asset data, proprietary procedures, regulatory requirements — cannot be routinely submitted to external AI services during this process.

Long-Term Platform Vision

Industrial knowledge and coordination environment.

The long-term vision for AxiOMSphere is an integrated industrial environment in which all AIMS processes function together as a goal-driven, continuously improving system — from project startup through full production operations.

In the long-term vision, the platform supports the full AIMS lifecycle: coordinating AIMS framework development across disciplines; maintaining consistency as scope, regulation and operating conditions evolve; retaining validated knowledge from every cycle; and supporting qualified specialists with traceable, evidence-referenced outputs at each decision point.

The system is designed to be self-orchestrating, self-monitoring, self-learning and self-restoring — operating within company objectives, approved strategic boundaries and qualified human governance. Every output remains a recommendation; the system augments specialist judgement, not replaces it.

This is a long-term vision, not a current product offering. The platform is being built progressively toward this vision through the staged milestones described above. Pilot deployment requires completion of controlled validation under formal data-handling agreements.
⚖️

Built for responsible industrial use.

AxiOMSphere develops and reviews AIMS management-system work products in accordance with ISO 55001 requirements and ISO 55002 guidance. Generated outputs and recommendations require qualified human review before operational use and do not replace formal assurance or certification activities.

Sensitive source information remains private by default. Approved external services receive anonymized context only unless separately authorised.
